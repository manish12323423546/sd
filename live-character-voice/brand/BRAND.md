# Altercast — brand guide

**Name:** Altercast (alter ego + broadcast): the broadcast, in the voice you choose.
**Descriptor:** Live Character Voice · **Tagline:** *Any commentary. Any character. Live.*
**Short pitch:** Altercast re-voices live sports commentary in character voices — in sync with
the picture, with the crowd kept — in any browser tab.

## Logo
- `mark.svg` — app icon / mark: a white "A" whose crossbar is a live sound wave, on the signal
  gradient squircle. `mark-small.svg` — 16 px version (no wave).
- `wordmark.svg` — mark + lowercase "altercast" in Sora Bold, tight tracking (−4/150).
- Clear space: ¼ of the mark's height on every side. Minimum size: 16 px (small mark), 24 px (mark).
- Don't recolour the A, rotate, stretch, outline, or put the mark on a busy photo without a panel.

## Type
Sora (headlines, 600–700, tight tracking) · Inter (UI text) · JetBrains Mono (numbers, timings).

## Voice
Confident, short, concrete. Measured numbers, never hype ("within 0.5 s", not "instant").
